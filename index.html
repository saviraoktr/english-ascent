Study principles

Keep the practice active

• Learn a grammar concept, then create your own examples.
• Retrieve vocabulary from memory before checking the meaning.
• Listen for meaning, then replay short sections and shadow them.
• Speak first without stopping; review errors afterwards.

• Read authentic material and notice how ideas are organised.
• Write regularly and revise for accuracy and clarity.
• Review recurring mistakes weekly.
• Record actual study time honestly.
